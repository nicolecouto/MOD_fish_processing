We have an EFE board in all FastCTDs and Epsi's - if nothing else it gives us the three acceleration channels on FCTD. If we have a microconductivity probe, that probe goes through an ADC on a sister board the sits on top of the EFE and then feeds into the s2 channel. The sister board is called the efe mezzanine. it has front end op-amp electronics.  The signal out of the op-amp travels down to EFE and the signal goes into the AUX± channel on the AD7124  ADC.  The FCTD configures that AD7124 so that it reads the aux± channel and not the epsi shear2 signal
